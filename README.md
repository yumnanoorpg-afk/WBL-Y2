1)Client business & initial
GrowMore Estate is a growing housing agency, and they are the client for this project. The Client contact is Zulkifal Sarhandi/co-founder of the agency. Email: info@growmoreestates.co.uk 
The initial meeting occurred on the add date Oct 2026. During the meeting a software solution was discussed to resolve the hassle of manually keeping a track of different properties and payments from the tenants.

2) Industry background
GrowMore acts as an in-between party of Landlords and the Buyers/Tenants. They are currently heavily relying on memory and notes. As the business grows, it will become increasing harder to keep a track of properties and payments. Due to this, they are looking for an efficient way to record and retrieve the information so they can monitor and take action on missed payments or other inquires. 

3) Proposed development 
The main objective of the project is to design and develop a web-based property tracking system Prototype to be used by the agents of GrowMore.
The system will allow the users to add or remove properties, keep a track on and update the status (to buy, to rent…. etc.) of properties, and track if payments are made or not. Properties will also be categorised in types in a database allow filtering to happen (will help filter best suited properties). A secure login system will also be developed for data privacy. 
The Prototype will be tested using realistic Beta Data to test functionality, accuracy and performance.
